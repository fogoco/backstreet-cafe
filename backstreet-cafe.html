Our Story

More than a coffee.
A place to belong.

Specialty coffee, house-made food, and warm hospitality — voted one of the Sunshine Coast's best cafes. Proudly serving Mooloolaba since 2016, with a passion for quality, local ingredients, and creating memorable experiences every day.

From our meticulously sourced beans to our seasonal menu built around local producers, every detail is intentional. Because we believe that great coffee and great food should be everyday luxuries.

Backstreet started in Buderim before moving to the Mooloolaba Esplanade, where you will find us today. We are walk-ins only — no bookings — so just come as you are.

Local First

Proudly partnering with Sunshine Coast producers who share our passion for quality, freshness and exceptional ingredients.

Specialty Coffee

Every cup is crafted with precision, from the dose we weigh to the tamp pressure, extraction time and water balance. Consistency isn't just our standard; it's what we're known for.

Seasonal Menu

Thoughtfully crafted dishes inspired by the seasons, celebrating fresh ingredients, local and simple flavours done exceptionally well.

Hospitality

Warm service, genuine connections and an experience designed to make every visit memorable.

Recognition

  • Named in the guide to the best Mooloolaba cafes for brunching. The Urban List, 2026
